# General-Analysis-Report-of-an-imaginary-retail-company.
This report was created as part of a Power BI course, and it shows metrics about sales and returns of an imaginary retail company.

a) The first page refers to the cover, where the user can find a link that leads to the main page.

b) The first page contains a general analysis of the company’s revenue and sales.

• In the upper part, cards with general information such as total revenue, total profit, total sales, and profit margin were used.
• A column chart was used to compare the revenue of the current year with the revenue of the previous year – this information helps to show the company’s growth over the past year.
• A donut chart was used to display the percentage of sales by store type (online stores vs physical stores).
• A bar chart shows the total revenue by product category. A tooltip provides more detailed information.
• A matrix table presents the total revenue, total profit, and total sales by continent. It allows the user to drill down and view all information by country as well.
• In the upper/right corner of the page, a slicer can be found that allows the user to filter the information by year.
The user can navigate to the other pages through the buttons placed in the upper/left side of the page.

c) On the next page, a detailed analysis of the total revenue by product can be found.
• In the upper part of the page, a scroller was placed to show the total revenue of each product category and its growth compared to the previous month.
• Right below the scroller, centered on the page, a decomposition tree displays the total revenue and total sales of each product – to switch between them, a parameter was created.
• At the bottom of the page, an Enlighten Data Story highlights which product and which store achieved the highest sales.
• A slicer can be found in the upper/right corner of the page to filter information by period, continent, and country.

d) The last page of the report contains information about returns. In the upper part of the page, cards are placed with information such as lost revenue, number of returns, percentage of lost revenue, and percentage of returns.
• A line chart shows the return percentage over the months – a tooltip provides detailed information.
• A map displays return data by continent.
• Finally, a column chart represents the percentage of returns, total sales, and total returns by brand.
• In the upper/right side of the page, a play axis can be found that allows the user to dynamically change the displayed product across all visuals on the page.
• On the upper/left side, the same slicer used on the first page can be used to filter the information by year.
To perform the necessary calculations for the visuals, DAX functions were used – revenue, profit, percentage of returns, etc.
Among the DAX functions used, we highlight:
Logical functions such as IF and SWITCH.
Mathematical functions such as SUM, AVERAGE, and MIN/MAX.
Context and Filter functions such as CALCULATE and ALL.
Iterator functions such as SUMX and AVERAGEX.
Time intelligence functions such as DATESYTD and DATESINPERIOD.
With this report, we were able to obtain detailed information about the company’s performance over time.
